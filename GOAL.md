# GOAL — COS Graph Engine

## North Star
Bring COS Graph Engine to a demonstrable **10/10 engineering standard across all 20 audited dimensions**, then qualify it as the zero-cost graph compute/projection and agent-runtime substrate of AGENTIC_SYSTEMS_OS.

## Definition of 10/10
A dimension is 10/10 only when its declared scope is supported by falsifiable guarantees and final machine evidence. Feature count, model confidence, documentation claims, raw test count, or a green badge alone are insufficient.

A 10/10 claim ultimately requires, where applicable:
- deterministic behavior;
- invariant/correctness evidence;
- negative and failure-path evidence;
- provenance and temporal correctness;
- permission/security enforcement;
- concurrency/idempotency guarantees;
- degraded-mode/resilience evidence;
- scientific performance evidence;
- replay/restore evidence;
- cold-start/recovery documentation.

## Authority Gate
Before W13 passes, COS is **non-authoritative** for AGENTIC_SYSTEMS_OS. Shadow/read/projection usage is allowed; canonical truth remains in the designated control plane.

## Cost constraint
Incremental recurring infrastructure cost must remain `EUR 0/month` unless the owner makes a new explicit decision.

## Non-goals during Convergence Era
- adding graph levels for breadth alone;
- hiding failures to make CI green;
- deleting history to simplify state;
- semantic identity merges without canonical evidence;
- automatic production deploy/release pipelines;
- introducing a paid graph database as a prerequisite.
